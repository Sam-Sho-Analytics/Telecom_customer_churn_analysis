Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

End-to-end telecom churn analysis using Power BI to identify drivers of customer churn and provide business insights and recommendations.

Project Overview

This project analyzes telecom customer churn to identify key factors that drive customer churn and provides business insights and recommendations to reduce churn rate.

Using Power BI, this analysis explores patterns in customer behavior, contract type, tenure, and monthly charges to determine the strongest predictors of churn.

Business Problem

Customer churn is a major challenge for telecom companies. Losing customers reduces recurring revenue and increases acquisition costs.

Goals:

  • Identify key drivers of churn
  • Find high-risk customer segments
  • Discover retention opportunities

Tools & Technologies

  • Power BI (Dashboard Development & Data Visualization)
  • Excel (Data Cleaning & Dataset Exploration)

Analytical Techniques

  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
  • Correlation Analysis
  • Customer Churn Segmentation
  • Business Intelligence Reporting

Dataset

The dataset contains telecom customer information including:

  • Customer tenure
  • Monthly charges
  • Contract type
  • Payment method
  • Internet service
  • Churn status

Key Insights

1. Contract Type

Customers with month-to-month contracts show the highest churn rate.

2. Tenure

Customers with shorter tenure show a high churn rate.

3. Monthly Charges

Higher monthly charges are correlated with increased churn.

4. Payment Method

Customers using electronic check payment method show higher churn rate.

Dashboard

Key Metrics

  • Total Customers
  • Churn Rate
  • Revenue lost to churn
  • Customer Segmentation

Business Recommendations

  1. Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts using discounts and incentives.
  2. Improve onboarding experience within the first 6 months to reduce early churn.
  3. Offer loyalty discounts for customers with high monthly charges.
